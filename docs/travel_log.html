📔 Field notes from the semester

A quick tour of the routes I actually traveled—each stop is a real repo on my GitHub (github.com/linarettig). No epic novel… just stamps in the passport and postcards from the trail.

🎒 What I packed (skills I reused everywhere)

  • Wrangling: dplyr habits that keep datasets from becoming a swamp
  • Viz: ggplot2 as my “camera” for evidence, not decoration
  • Space: sf lessons (CRS is a mood, but you can manage it)
  • Automation: map()-style thinking from the iteration repo
  • Storytelling: Quarto + GitHub as proof I didn’t just visit the data—I left breadcrumbs

🧳 Souvenirs (tiny memories, big lessons)

  • Debugging-AI: The trail teaches you that the fastest “shortcut” is a reproducible tiny example.
  • APIs-Functions-Iteration: The moment functions clicked felt like upgrading from a paper map to GPS.
  • Geospatial: My first “real map” souvenir—worth framing if CRS behaved.
  • Nested-Modeling: When one model becomes many models… organization becomes survival gear.
  • Colorado-Air-Quality-Index: Clear plots beat clever plots when someone asks “so what?”
  • r_finalproject: Connecting visitation + air felt like linking two trails into one vista.
